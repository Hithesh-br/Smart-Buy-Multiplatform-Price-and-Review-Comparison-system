Smart-Buy Ecosystem

About SmartBuy

SmartBuy is a multi-platform product price and review comparison system that helps users compare products across multiple online shopping platforms. Users can search for products using product names or specifications and compare current prices, ratings, reviews, and available product details from different websites.

Key Features

Multi-platform product search

Search products simultaneously across top Indian e-commerce sites.

Product specification search

Filter products by category, RAM, Storage, Processor, Brand, and Price.

Amazon price comparison

Real-time scraped pricing, stock, and ratings directly from Amazon India.

Flipkart price comparison

Instant side-by-side deal extraction and offer ratings from Flipkart.

Meesho price comparison

Discover competitive prices and budget offers live from Meesho.

Best price detection

Automatic identification of the absolute lowest valid price per platform.

Product specification comparison

Interactive side-by-side spec comparison table for all matching products.

Ratings and reviews comparison

Consolidated customer review counts and rating badges across stores.

Best overall deal recommendation

Smart savings calculator highlights the top product deal and calculates exact savings compared to other platforms.
